System Plus College Foundation

Benefits:

  • Full or partial tuition fee discounts
  • Free miscellaneous and laboratory fees (for some grants)
  • Monthly stipend (for specific grants)
  • Book allowance (if applicable)

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a Filipino citizen
  • Must be officially enrolled in SPCF
  • Must meet academic performance requirements (e.g., no failing grades)
  • Must have good moral character
  • Must pass the screening or tryout (for athletic or cultural scholarships)
  • Additional qualifications depending on the scholarship category

Types of SPCF Scholarships:

  • Academic Scholarship – for students with high academic performance
  • Athletic Scholarship – for student-athletes competing in school sports
  • Cultural Scholarship – for those with talent in arts, music, dance, or theater
  • SPCF Grant-in-Aid Program – for financially challenged students
  • Leadership Scholarship – for student organization officers
  • External Scholarships – including those from CHED, DOST, and private partners

Requirements:

  • Accomplished SPCF Scholarship Application Form
  • Latest academic grades or transcript
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Barangay Clearance or Certificate of Indigency (for financial grants)
  • Income documents (if applicable)
  • 2x2 ID photos
  • Proof of achievements (for cultural, academic, or athletic scholarships)
