Benefits:

  • Full tuition fee coverage
  • Monthly stipend

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Filipino citizen
  • Incoming freshmen or college student in a partner school

  • Must be enrolled or planning to enroll in a partner university and an approved course

  • Engineering (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, etc.)
  • Architecture
  • Information Technology / Computer Science
  • Accountancy
  • Management / Business-related courses
  • Communication and Marketing courses
  • Education

Requirements:

  • Application form (downloadable via Megaworld Foundation site)
  • Income documents (ITR, Certificate of Indigency, etc.)
  • Certified true copy of grades
  • Recommendation letter from school principal or guidance counselor
  • Birth certificate
  • Recent 2x2 ID picture
  • Proof of enrollment or admission (for incoming freshmen)
