GBF teachSTEM College Scholarship

Benefits:

  • Full tuition and other school fees (for partner schools)
  • Fixed monthly allowance
  • Book and instructional materials allowance
  • Professional development training (STEM-focused)
  • Opportunities for community engagement and mentoring
  • Priority for GBF employment and partner networks

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a Filipino citizen
  • Must be enrolled or enrolling in a Bachelor's degree in Education major in Science, Mathematics, Technology or related STEM areas
  • Committed to teaching in public K–12 schools after graduation
  • With good academic standing (GWA varies per program, typically ≥ 85%)
  • Must have no disciplinary record
  • Must demonstrate leadership potential and community involvement

Requirements:

  • Completed online application form
  • Transcript of Records or copy of grades
  • Certificate of Enrollment (if currently enrolled)
  • Recommendation letters
  • Proof of income or Certificate of Indigency (if applicable)
  • Short essay or video answering specific application questions
  • Valid ID and 2×2 photo
