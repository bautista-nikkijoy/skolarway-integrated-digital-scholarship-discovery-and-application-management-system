SkolarWay

Angeles University Foundation

Benefits:

  • Full or partial tuition fee coverage
  • Miscellaneous fee discounts
  • Book allowance (for select programs)
  • Monthly stipend (for certain scholarships)
  • Priority enrollment and academic advising

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a Filipino citizen
  • Must meet the academic, financial, or talent-based qualifications of the specific scholarship
  • Must be enrolled at AUF in an undergraduate or graduate program
  • Must comply with grade and conduct requirements as stated by AUF’s scholarship policies

Types of AUF Scholarships:

  • Academic Scholarship – for students with outstanding academic performance
  • Athletic Scholarship – for student-athletes competing in AUF varsity teams
  • Cultural Scholarship – for talents in dance, music, and theater
  • Leadership Scholarship – for student leaders with active involvement in organizations
  • Financial Assistance Grant – for students with proven financial need
  • External-funded Scholarships – from CHED, private companies, and NGOs in partnership with AUF

Requirements:

  • Duly accomplished AUF Scholarship Application Form
  • Latest academic grades (report card or transcript)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Recommendation letter (if required)
  • Proof of income (ITR, Certificate of Indigency, etc.)
  • Portfolio or tryout (for cultural or athletic scholarships)
  • Other documents as specified per scholarship type
