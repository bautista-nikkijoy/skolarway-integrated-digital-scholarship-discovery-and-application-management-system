📘 What is SkolarWay?

SkolarWay is a mobile app designed to help incoming college students in Pampanga discover available scholarships. It shows you:

  • Scholarships you may be eligible for
  • Requirements and contract terms
  • Application deadlines
  • Instructions on how and where to apply

🎓 Who is SkolarWay for?

  • Incoming 1st year college students
  • Students based in Pampanga
  • Those looking for financial aid or academic scholarships

🔐 Data & Privacy

  • Your data is only used to match you with scholarships.
  • We don’t share your personal information with third parties.
