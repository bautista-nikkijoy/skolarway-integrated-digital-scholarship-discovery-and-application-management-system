Overseas Workers Welfare Administration

Benefits:

  • Education for Development Scholarship Program (EDSP): Financial aid of up to ₱60,000 per school year for qualifying dependents who place among the top examinees of the DOST‑SEI pre‑qualifying exam, for both incoming freshmen and continuing college students Phil Scholar.
  • Congressional Migrant Workers Scholarship Program (CMWSP): Scholarship grants (up to ₱60,000/year) for active OFWs or their immediate descendants pursuing Science & Technology courses; includes a national qualifying exam by DOST‑SEI OWWA Scholarships.
  • OFW Dependent Scholarship Program (ODSP):Cash assistance of ₱20,000 per academic year for dependent children or siblings of active OWWA members enrolled (or planning to enroll) in any 4‑ or 5‑year baccalaureate course OWWA Scholarships.
  • Education and Livelihood Assistance Program (ELAP):Support for dependents of deceased OFW members to pursue vocational and livelihood training (amounts and terms vary by program).

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be an active OWWA member (OFW) or their child/sibling (single, no prior scholarship avails).
  • General income ceiling: family monthly income not exceeding USD 600 (≈ ₱34,000) for most dependent programs OWWA Scholarships.
  • Age limits: Incoming freshmen: ≤ 21 years old or Continuing students: ≤ 30 years old.​
  • Academic requirements: EDSP applicants must rank in the DOST‑SEI Top 1000 examinees or CMWSP & ODSP applicants must maintain a GWA ≥ 80% or equivalent and no failing grades.
  • Must enroll in a Philippine‑based college/university offering the qualified course.

Requirements:

  • Accomplished OWWA Scholarship Application Form
  • PSA Birth Certificate
  • Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR) or proof of single status (for dependents)
  • 2×2 ID photo
  • Proof of relationship to OFW (e.g., OWWA Member ID)
  • Parent’s/Guardian’s Income Tax Return (ITR) or Certificate of Indigency
  • Academic records: Form 137/138 or certified transcript of grades (including Grade 9–11 for CMWSP) OWWA Scholarships.
  • Additional for CMWSP: Copy of OFW passport, proof of family income, and DOST‑SEI exam result.
  • Additional for ODSP: Proof of enrollment or admission in a 4–5‑year course.
