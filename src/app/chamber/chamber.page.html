Pampanga Chamber of Commerce and Industry

Benefits:

  • Interest‑Free Loan Assistance: Awarded to qualifying micro‑entrepreneurs to help cover start‑up or operating capital (47 enterprises served as of April 2025) Pampanga Chamber.
  • Entrepreneurship Training & Workshops: Capacity‑building seminars on business planning, marketing, and finance.
  • Networking & Mentorship: Access to regular mixers, trade missions, and one‑on‑one guidance from established business leaders.
  • Advocacy & Policy Support: Representation in local and national policy discussions to protect and advance MSME interests.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a bona fide Pampanga resident operating a micro‑ or small‑scale enterprise.
  • Business must be formally registered or in the process of registration.
  • Active membership (or willingness to join) in PamCham.​

Requirements:

  • Completed PamCham Assistance Application Form.
  • Proof of business registration (DTI/SEC/Cooperative).
  • Barangay Certificate of Residency.
  • Brief business proposal or project plan.
  • Latest financial statements or cash‑flow projections.
