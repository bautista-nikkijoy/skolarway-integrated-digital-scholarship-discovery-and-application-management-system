HOLY ANGEL UNIVERSITY

Benefits:

  • Full or partial tuition and miscellaneous fee coverage
  • Monthly stipend or book allowance
  • Access to special programs, training, or mentoring
  • Priority registration privileges
  • Recognition and awards

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a Filipino citizen
  • Must be officially enrolled in any undergraduate or graduate program at HAU
  • Must meet the specific GPA or performance qualifications required by the scholarship
  • Must demonstrate good moral character
  • Must pass evaluation, audition, tryouts, or interview (if applicable)

Types of HAU Scholarships:

  • Entrance Scholarships – for incoming freshmen with high honors
  • Academic Scholarships – for current students with excellent academic standing
  • Athletic Scholarships – for varsity athletes
  • Artistic/Cultural Scholarships – for talents in music, theater, and arts
  • Financial Aid Grants – for students from low-income families
  • Leadership Scholarships – for student government and organization leaders
  • Service Grants – for student assistants
  • External-funded Scholarships – CHED, DOST, LGUs, and private partners

Requirements:

  • Completed HAU Scholarship Application Form
  • Latest academic records or report card
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Recent 2x2 photo
  • Proof of income or Certificate of Indigency (for financial assistance)
  • Portfolio, audition video, or tryout results (for arts/athletics)
  • Recommendation letter (for leadership or academic awards)
