DOST-SEI Undergraduate Scholarship

Benefits:

  • Tuition and other school fees: ₱40,000/year
  • Book Allowance: ₱10,000/year
  • Monthly Living Allowance: ₱7,000/month
  • Transportation Allowance (for those studying outside home region)
  • Thesis Allowance: ₱10,000
  • Graduation Clothing Allowance: ₱1,000
  • Group Health and Accident Insurance

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Natural-born Filipino citizen
  • Graduating Grade 12 student from a recognized school
  • Must belong to the STEM strand (for RA 7687 and Merit) or top 5% of the graduating class (for non-STEM)
  • Must be in good health and of good moral character
  • Annual family income ≤ ₱400,000
  • Must pursue a DOST-SEI priority S&T course at a recognized university
  • RA 7687 applicants must have a family gross income not exceeding the DOST-set cut-off

Requirements:

  • DOST-SEI Application Form
  • Certificate of good moral character
  • Certificate of Good Health
  • Principal’s Certification (ranking or STEM strand)
  • Birth Certificate (PSA copy)
  • Recent 2×2 ID photo
  • Parent’s/Guardian’s Income Tax Return (ITR) or Certificate of Indigency
  • School records (Form 137, report card, etc.)
