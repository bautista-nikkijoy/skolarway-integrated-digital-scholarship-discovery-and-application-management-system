University of the Assumption

Benefits:

  • Full or partial tuition fee discounts
  • Waiver of miscellaneous and laboratory fees
  • Monthly stipend or book allowance
  • Access to leadership and spiritual formation programs
  • Priority consideration for honors and recognition

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Filipino citizen
  • Must be officially enrolled at the University of the Assumption
  • Must meet specific grade or performance requirements depending on the scholarship
  • Must have no disciplinary record
  • Must comply with the formation activities and scholarship policies of UA

Types of UA Scholarships:

  • Academic Scholarship – for students with outstanding academic performance
  • Athletic Scholarship – for students who are part of UA varsity teams
  • Cultural Scholarship – for students active in performing arts (choir, dance troupe, etc.)
  • Religious Vocation Scholarship – for seminarians and religious aspirants
  • Financial Assistance Grant – for students from low-income families
  • Leadership Scholarship – for student leaders in organizations or councils
  • External Scholarships – CHED, DOST, LGUs, and private sector partners

Requirements:

  • Accomplished UA Scholarship Application Form
  • Latest grades or transcript
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • 2x2 ID photos
  • Proof of income or Certificate of Indigency (for financial grants)
  • Recommendation letter (if required)
  • Portfolio, audition, or tryout (for cultural or athletic applicants)
