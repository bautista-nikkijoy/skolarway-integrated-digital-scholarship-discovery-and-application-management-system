City College Of San Fernando Scholarship and Grants

Benefits:

  • Free tuition and miscellaneous fees
  • Monthly allowance or stipend (for select grants)
  • Book and uniform allowance (depending on the program)
  • Access to leadership and academic development opportunities
  • Priority for city-sponsored programs and job placement assistance

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a bonafide resident of the City of San Fernando, Pampanga
  • Must be officially enrolled at CCSF
  • Must meet the academic or performance qualifications required by the specific scholarship
  • Must not have any disciplinary record
  • Family’s annual gross income must meet the program’s income threshold

Types of Scholarships and Grants:

  • Academic Scholarship – for high-performing students with excellent grades
  • Financial Assistance Grant – for students from indigent families
  • Leadership and Service Grant – for student leaders or those with active community involvement
  • Athletic and Talent Grant – for students with skills in sports or performing arts
  • Special Government-funded Programs – in partnership with CHED, LGU, or NGOs

Requirements:

  • Completed Scholarship Application Form
  • Barangay Certificate of Residency
  • Proof of income (ITR or Certificate of Indigency)
  • Latest report card or transcript of records
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • 2x2 ID photo
  • Other documents depending on the specific grant applied for
