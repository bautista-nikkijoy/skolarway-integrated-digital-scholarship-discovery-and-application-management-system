Educational Financial Assistance Program (EFAP)

Benefits:

  • Cash assistance of ₱4,000 per beneficiary per distribution (semester/term) SunStarPhilippine Information Agency.
  • For SY 2021–2022, Grade 12 beneficiaries collectively received ₱57,515,000 SunStar.
  • Additional support available via the Alternative Learning System for out‑of‑school youth.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a bona fide resident of Pampanga.
  • Officially enrolled in a CHED‑ or DepEd‑recognized senior high school or college institution within Pampanga.
  • Family assessed as indigent per provincial guidelines (barangay certification or Certificate of Indigency).​
  • Must maintain good academic standing throughout the term SunStar.​

Requirements:

  • Accomplished EFAP Application Form.
  • Proof of residency (Barangay Certificate).
  • Proof of enrollment (school ID or Certification of Registration).
  • Proof of income or Certificate of Indigency.
  • PSA Birth Certificate.
  • 2×2 ID photo.
