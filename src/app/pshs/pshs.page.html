PSHS Grade 7 Scholarship National Competitive Examination

Benefits:

  • Free tuition and laboratory fees
  • Monthly stipend
  • Free loan of textbooks
  • Uniform and transportation subsidy (as applicable)
  • Living allowance for low-income families
  • Dormitory (in select campuses)
  • Access to advanced STEM curriculum

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a Filipino citizen
  • Currently enrolled in Grade 6
  • Must belong to the top 10% of the graduating class or must have a final grade of 85% or higher in Science and Math
  • Must be in good health and fit to undergo a rigorous academic program
  • Must not have taken the NCE previously
  • Must not have applied for admission to PSHS through any backdoor or special admission scheme

Requirements:

  • Completed NCE Application Form
  • Certificate of Academic Ranking (or certified grades)
  • Certified copy of birth certificate
  • Two recent 1×1 ID photos
  • Proof of income (for stipend and subsidy eligibility)
  • Valid school ID or equivalent document
