Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS)

Benefits:

  • Medical Assistance: Hospitalization, medicines, diagnostics, assistive devices
  • Funeral (Burial) Assistance
  • Transportation Assistance
  • Educational Assistance (school fees, supplies)
  • Food Assistance (food packs)
  • Cash Assistance for Other Support Services
  • Material Assistance: Family food packs, hygiene/sleeping kits, assistive technologies
  • Psychosocial Support: Counseling, Psychological First Aid
  • Referral Services to other agencies Financial Assistance Philippines.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Filipino citizen or legal resident facing a “state of active crisis” as assessed by a DSWD Social Worker
  • Individuals or families in crisis (medical emergencies, natural disasters, loss of livelihood, death in the family, extreme poverty, etc.)
  • Financially incapacitated (as defined under RA 11463) may be prioritized, though income status is one of several factors.​

Requirements:

  • Accomplished AICS Application Form
  • Proof of crisis (e.g., Medical Certificate/Abstract/Discharge Summary for medical aid; Death Certificate for burial aid; School Records/Form 137 for educational aid)
  • PSA Birth Certificate or valid ID
  • Barangay Certificate or Certificate of Indigency (to establish financial need)
  • 2×2 ID photo
